# Milestone Recipes V1

Status: **MECHANICAL RECIPE BASELINE — VALUES ARE PLACEHOLDERS FOR NEXT BALANCE PASS**

Purpose: make long-term progression depend on the production system the player has built, not only on accumulating cash.

Exact amounts will change. Dependency structure is more important than the current numbers.

## Recipe philosophy

Major progression generally has two parts:
1. cash / purchased asset;
2. player-produced fit-out/modules.

This prevents a wealthy player from skipping the entire workshop while still preserving the Buy-vs-Build philosophy for ordinary machines.

Example:
- player can buy a used Truck chassis;
- player still fabricates/installs its salvage Drive / Control / Winch systems.

## Early reference milestones

### Small Hydraulic Press
Already established early-game baseline:
- 100 kg metal;
- Bearings x2;
- Hydraulic Unit x1;
- ~2,500 cash/consumables;
- fabrication time.

Purpose:
- first machine;
- metal briquettes;
- proof of Build vs Buy.

### Wire Stripper
Recipe family:
- metal;
- Electric Motor;
- Electronic Module;
- cash/consumables.

Purpose:
- creates **Clean Copper**;
- Clean Copper becomes a construction input rather than merely a higher sale price.

### Furnace / Metalworking Cell
Target: later Garage before Hangar.

Recipe family:
- metal;
- Hydraulic / Motor components;
- Electronic Module;
- some Clean Copper;
- possibly Compressor depending final machine identity.

Purpose:
- Steel Blanks;
- Aluminium Billets;
- later upgraded industrial furnace produces Alloy Billets.

The Furnace must be available early enough that Hangar fit-out can consume processed structural materials.

## Van milestone

Target band: 8–15 progression hours.

### Acquisition
Preferred V1:
- buy a used Van chassis for cash (~70k current model placeholder);
- requires appropriate progression/garage/storage milestone;
- no requirement to "craft a whole van from scrap".

### Salvage fit-out
Player fabricates one or more modules at Workbench II.

#### Cargo Rack
Recipe family:
- metal;
- Bearings;
- Aluminium later optional for upgraded version.

Effect:
- mobile bulk capacity.

#### Component Locker
Recipe family:
- metal / Plastic Granulate;
- Electronic Module optional for later smart version.

Effect:
- protected/discrete rare-component storage.

#### Quick-unload kit
Recipe family:
- Bearings;
- metal;
- later small Motor.

Effect:
- faster Cart -> Van transfer.

The base Van should still work without every module; modules are progression, not mandatory onboarding chores.

## First worker milestone

Target: 12–20h, always after Van.

Requirements:
- Van already owned;
- at least Z1 mastered;
- one staff slot at base;
- cash onboarding/hiring cost (~35k current model placeholder).

First worker receives Basic Route Kit rather than permanently taking the player's exciting new Van.

## Full Garage milestone

Target: 18–30h.

Represents final practical expansion before Hangar pressure.

Recipe/investment family:
- cash for shelves/power/floor work;
- ordinary metal;
- Clean Copper for electrical fit-out;
- Electronic Module;
- Electric Motor or Pump for workshop infrastructure.

Purpose:
- storage increase;
- second worker slot;
- final Garage machine footprints;
- visible 8/8 fullness problem.

## Hangar milestone

Target: 28–50h.

This must be a large goal.

### Property / lease / acquisition
- substantial cash (~220k current structural model placeholder).

### Workshop fit-out
Requires outputs the player cannot simply loot as finished items:
- Steel Blanks;
- Aluminium Billets;
- Clean Copper;
- **Structural Machine Frame** x1–2;
- **Control Module** x1;
- ordinary bulk metal for racks/floor/storage.

Possible input components behind those modules:
- Bearings;
- Motors;
- Electronics;
- Gearbox depending chosen recipe version.

Why this matters:

A player can have enough cash to acquire the building but still needs their workshop to turn salvage into the infrastructure that makes it usable.

Unlocks:
- larger storage;
- worker/vehicle area;
- Sorting Conveyor;
- advanced Furnace;
- Truck fit-out recipes;
- Tier II Workshop/Automation depth.

## Truck milestone

Target: roughly 38–65h structural band.

### Acquisition
- used Truck chassis purchased for cash (~180–300k range to sensitivity-test; V0 model currently uses 180k cash-equivalent).

### Mandatory salvage conversion
At least:
- **Drive Module** x1;
- **Control Module** x1;
- Steel/Aluminium structural material;
- Gearbox or Generator/Alternator family;
- Clean Copper.

Optional modules:
- Powered Winch;
- larger component cage;
- mobile tool-power system;
- route/crew module.

Result:
- player gains industrial mobile depot;
- former Van becomes assignable to worker routes.

## Salvage Yard milestone

Target: 45–80h.

The Yard is an outdoor expansion around/adjacent to the Hangar.

Investment family:
- cash / land expansion;
- Structural Machine Frames x2+;
- Hydraulic Power Module x1;
- Steel Blanks;
- bulk metal;
- Control Module;
- storage bins / vehicle bays.

Unlocks:
- heavy-object staging;
- more worker/vehicle slots;
- Truck routes;
- Heavy Fabrication Station;
- bulk storage;
- Z6 Abandoned Factory progression.

## Heavy Fabrication Station

Key mid/late machine.

Requires:
- Structural Frame;
- Drive Module;
- Hydraulic Power Module;
- Control Module;
- Alloy Billets;
- cash/consumables.

Purpose:
- advanced vehicle modules;
- industrial machine frames;
- Recovery Rig components;
- Industrial Workshop construction.

This machine is one of the main sinks for advanced processed material and recovered industrial components.

## Industrial Workshop milestone

Target: 75–120h.

Investment family:
- cash (~650k cash-equivalent placeholder in V0 model);
- Structural Frames x3–5;
- Drive Modules x2;
- Control Modules x2;
- Hydraulic Power Module x1–2;
- Alloy Billets;
- Clean Copper;
- heavy industrial components discovered in Z5/Z6.

Unlocks:
- production cells;
- advanced worker roles / operators / technicians;
- Tier III Workshop/Automation;
- Recovery Rig fit-out;
- continuous configured production.

## Recovery Rig / Heavy Hauler

Preferred acquisition:
- purchase industrial vehicle/chassis;
- fabricate salvage-specific conversion.

Required module families:
- Heavy Hydraulic Cylinder;
- Hydraulic Power Module;
- Drive Module;
- Control Module;
- Alloy structural parts;
- Gearbox / Generator depending vehicle design.

Purpose:
- enables selected heavy Z7/Z8 targets;
- large route buffer;
- late worker route use when player eventually moves to next transport tier.

## Processing Plant milestone

Target: 120h+.

Large investment rather than single recipe.

Structural V1 requirement family:
- major cash investment (~1.2m cash-equivalent placeholder in V0 structural model; sensitivity required);
- multiple Structural Machine Frames;
- multiple Drive Modules;
- multiple Control Modules;
- multiple Hydraulic Power Modules;
- Steel/Alloy/Aluminium processed material;
- Industrial Controllers;
- Heavy Hydraulic Cylinders;
- high-tier worker/zone milestones.

Plant must also require:
- several zones mastered;
- Industrial Workshop operational;
- at least one mature worker/vehicle route.

This prevents pure-cash rush.

Unlocks:
- industrial lines;
- huge storage;
- crew/cell management;
- large industrial contracts;
- Port/Container late frontier.

## Anti-skip rule

Cash may accelerate ordinary station acquisition, but at least some major milestones require player-produced modules.

The intended feeling is:

`I finally found the gearbox -> now I can fabricate the Drive Module -> now my Truck conversion is possible.`

—not:

`I idled enough cash, clicked Buy Truck, and every previous system stopped mattering.`
