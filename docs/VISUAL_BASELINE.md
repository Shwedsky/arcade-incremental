# Approved Visual Baseline V1

Status: **OWNER APPROVED**

The approved concept is a split visual direction covering both major screens: active scavenging and the roofless garage/workshop.

## A. Scavenging map

The screen should communicate, at a glance:
- compact top-down / light pseudo-2.5D salvage zone;
- simple readable character with a hand cart;
- dense field of breakable targets;
- automatic dismantling of the nearby valid target;
- strong break feedback;
- multiple raw-resource pickups bursting outward;
- magnet pickup into the cart;
- cart load / weight visibly increasing;
- resource counters visibly increasing;
- rare component drop with much stronger emphasis than common materials;
- later Combo state can intensify particles, pickup speed and overall momentum.

The approved concept intentionally prioritizes the feeling of a washing machine/fridge/junk pile **breaking into value** over realistic object detail.

## B. Garage / workshop

The garage is shown from above without a roof and must feel like a physical place, not only a menu.

Required presentation:
- manual workbench;
- storage racks / stock area;
- installed Small Compactor Press;
- recognizable simplified machine silhouettes;
- empty/locked future machine locations with padlocks;
- selected machine highlighted physically in the room;
- machine information panel showing input, output, throughput/queue, effect and next upgrade;
- locked-machine panel showing exact requirements and where missing parts can be found or purchased.

The garage should become visibly busier over time until the player understands, before a tooltip says so, that the space is becoming too small and a hangar is the next major goal.

## Style guardrails

- modern clean indie/mobile visual language;
- readable silhouettes and effects before fine detail;
- restrained character animation scope;
- no need to model faces, anatomy, clothing physics or realistic machinery internals;
- use art budget on destruction, drops, counters, rare-component moments, Combo escalation and visible workshop growth.
